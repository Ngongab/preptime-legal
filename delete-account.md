# Delete Your Prep Time Account

**Prep Time** allows you to permanently delete your account and all associated data directly from the app. Follow the steps below.

## How to delete your account

1. Open the **Prep Time** app on your device.
2. Go to **Settings** (tap the gear icon).
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete Account**.
4. Re-authenticate to confirm your identity:
   - **Email/password users** — enter your account password.
   - **Google Sign-In users** — authenticate with your Google account.
5. Confirm the deletion. Your account will be permanently removed.

## What data is deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is **permanently deleted** and cannot be recovered:

- Your account profile (email address, display name, profile photo)
- Exam preferences (exam board, grade, subjects)
- Quiz history and answers
- Flashcard decks and progress
- Notes you created
- Study progress and statistics
- Push notification tokens and notification history
- Any active subscription or purchase association with your account

## What data is retained

- **Anonymised, aggregated analytics** (e.g. crash reports collected by Firebase Crashlytics) that cannot be linked back to you may be retained for up to 90 days for app stability purposes.
- No personally identifiable information is kept after deletion.

## How long deletion takes

Account deletion is processed **immediately** upon confirmation. All personal data is removed from our servers in real time.

## Need help?

If you are unable to access the app or need assistance deleting your account, contact us at **zedindiehacker@gmail.com**. Please include the email address associated with your account so we can verify your identity and process the request.
